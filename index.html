Hydrate with your friends.

Add a friend with a six-letter code, send a cheer when they need one, and take on a friend challenge together. Everyone works toward their own goal, so it's teamwork, never a race.

Bestie streak
Keep a streak going together: every day you both hit your goal.
Squad week
Up to 6 of you, each hitting your own goal on 5 of the next 7 days.
Refill relay
Fill one big shared bottle together this week.
Twinning day
Match colorways for a day and both hit your goal.
Morning sip pact
Both log a refill before 9am on 5 of the next 7 days.

Finish one together and you both get a gold-edition sticker. Anyone can join a challenge for free, and starting one is part of the Sip Squad.

Glossy, waterproof NFC stickers made to work on metal bottles like Owala and Stanley. Stick one on, set it up once, then tap your phone to it whenever you drink.
