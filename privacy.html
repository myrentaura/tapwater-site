Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

Tap Water: One-Tap Tracker ("Tap Water", "we") is a water tracking app. This policy explains what the app and this website do with your information. The short version: your water log stays on your phone.

The app

  • No account. You never sign up or sign in.
  • Your data stays on your device. Your glasses, goal, settings, streaks and stickers are stored only on your phone. We don't have a server, so we never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. The app contains no advertising or analytics code and does not share data with third parties.
  • Notifications are scheduled on your phone. Logging a glass from a notification happens on your phone.
  • NFC bottle stickers. When you set up a sticker, the app writes a short link (tapwater://log) to it. Tapping the sticker opens Tap Water and logs a glass. The sticker holds no personal information.
  • Cheers. When you send a cheer, the app makes a link and picture containing the message and the name you type, and you choose where to send it with your phone's share menu. It never passes through us.
  • Purchases. The Extras subscription is bought through the App Store or Google Play, which handle your payment under their own privacy policies. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active; it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts, not your name, email or water log. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Sticker orders on this website

Sticker packs are paid for through Stripe. Stripe collects your payment details, and shares your name, email and shipping address with us so we can send your order and contact you about it. We keep these details only as long as needed for orders, returns and tax records, and never sell them. Stripe's privacy policy is at stripe.com/privacy.

Referral codes are Stripe promotion codes. When a code is used, we can see which code was used on an order so we can send the referrer their free sticker.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement. The site has no cookies, ads or analytics of its own.

Children

Tap Water is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Questions? See the help page for how to reach us.
