Help

Answers to common questions. If yours isn't here, get in touch at the bottom of the page.

How do I set up a bottle sticker?

Stick it on your bottle, open Tap Water, go to Settings and choose Set up a sticker. Hold the top of your phone to the sticker until it says it's ready. From then on, tapping your phone to the sticker logs a refill.

My phone doesn't react to the sticker

  • Make sure NFC is turned on in your phone's settings.
  • Hold the sticker to the top back of your phone (most phones' NFC reader is there) for a second.
  • Phone cases with metal plates or magnets can block NFC.
  • On iPhone, the phone must be unlocked. iPhone XS and newer read stickers without opening the app first.

I'm not getting reminders

Check that reminders are on in Tap Water's Settings and that notifications are allowed for Tap Water in your phone's settings. Battery saver can delay notifications on some phones.

I logged by mistake

Tap Undo last refill on the Today screen.

How do I cancel or manage Extras?

Subscriptions are managed by your phone's store. On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play, your profile, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions.

How do I get Extras back on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, open Settings in Tap Water and choose Restore purchases.

Is my water log backed up?

Your log is stored only on your phone, so it isn't copied anywhere. We don't have an account system or a server.

Sticker orders

You'll get an email receipt from Stripe after ordering. If something is wrong with your order, reply with your order number and we'll make it right.
