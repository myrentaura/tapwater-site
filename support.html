Help

Answers to common questions. If yours isn't here, get in touch at the bottom of the page.

How do I set up a bottle sticker?

There's nothing to set up. Stick it on your bottle near the top and tap your phone to it. The first tap asks what it's on: your bottle, a glass or another bottle size. After that, every tap logs one refill of that size. You can change or remove a sticker in Settings under My stickers.

Will any NFC sticker work?

No. Only tapwater™ stickers log refills. Each one carries its own code from our factory, so other stickers, cards and tags are ignored.

My phone doesn't react to the sticker

  • Make sure NFC is turned on in your phone's settings.
  • Hold the sticker to the top back of your phone (most phones' NFC reader is there) for a second.
  • Phone cases with metal plates or magnets can block NFC.
  • On iPhone, the phone must be unlocked. iPhone XS and newer read stickers without opening the app first.

I'm not getting reminders

Check that reminders are on in tapwater™'s Settings and that notifications are allowed for tapwater™ in your phone's settings. Battery saver can delay notifications on some phones.

I logged by mistake

Press and hold a refill on the Today screen to remove it. To fix an earlier day, open History, tap that day, and remove a refill or add one you missed.

How do I cancel or manage my Sip Squad membership?

Subscriptions are managed by your phone's store. On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play, your profile, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions. In tapwater™, How to cancel in Settings takes you straight there. Cancel at least 24 hours before your free trial or current period ends and you won't be charged again.

How do I get my Sip Squad membership back on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, open Settings in tapwater™ and choose Restore purchases.

Is my water log backed up?

Your log is stored only on your phone, so it isn't copied anywhere. We don't have an account system or a server.

Sticker orders
