Using your bottle sticker

  1. Place a sticker on your bottle. Take a tapwater™ sticker from the front of your card. Find a clean, dry spot on your bottle and press firmly for 10 seconds.
  2. Tap your phone to it. Hold the top of your phone to the sticker. The first tap sets your bottle size.
  3. Then every tap logs a refill.

Make sure NFC is turned on in your phone's settings. More help
